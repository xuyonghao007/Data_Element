📝 C4: 审稿意见回复(Rebuttal Letter)写作

从Major Revision到Accept — 掌握回复的艺术与策略

正确面对审稿意见的心态

首先要知道：收到修改意见（即使是Major Revision）已经是好消息了！

  • 🎉 大多数期刊的初审拒稿率超过70%，能进入修改环节说明编辑认可你工作的基本价值
  • 💪 审稿人的意见即使尖锐，也是在帮助你改进论文——他们的时间也是宝贵的
  • 🧘 情绪管理：收到意见后先放24小时再读，避免情绪化回应
  • 📋 逐条拆解：把每条意见分类（易解决/需补充实验/需重新论证/难以满足）

审稿意见的常见类型

补充文献/讨论

"请补充XX方面的文献讨论"、"建议引用YYY的最新研究"

容易 · 1-2天

补充实证检验

"建议增加XX作为控制变量"、"请做额外的稳健性检验"

中等 · 3-7天

方法论质疑

"识别策略存在内生性问题"、"DID的平行趋势假设存疑"

较难 · 1-2周

质疑核心贡献

"创新性不足"、"与ZZZ(2022)过于相似"、"研究问题不够重要"

困难 · 需要重大修改

Rebuttal Letter 标准格式模板

逐条回复模板

格式：原文意见 + 回应 + 修改说明
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Comment #1 [Reviewer 1, Point 2]:
"The authors should discuss the potential endogeneity concern 
more thoroughly, especially regarding reverse causality."

Response:
We thank the reviewer for raising this important point. We agree that 
endogeneity is a key concern in this literature. To address this issue, 
we have made the following improvements:

① In Section 3.2 (page 12), we added a detailed discussion of 
   potential endogeneity sources and our identification strategy.

② Following the reviewer's suggestion, we now employ an instrumental 
   variable approach using [describe IV] as an instrument for our core 
   explanatory variable. The first-stage F-statistic is 23.4, well above 
   the conventional threshold of 10, suggesting a strong instrument.

③ The IV results are reported in new Table A3 (Appendix), which shows 
   that our main findings remain qualitatively unchanged.

Changes in manuscript:
- Page 12, Paragraph 3: Added discussion of endogeneity concerns
- Appendix: New Table A3 with IV estimation results
- Page 8, Line 5: Revised to acknowledge this limitation more explicitly
当你不同意审稿人意见时
Comment #2 [Reviewer 2, Point 1]:
"The authors should include firm fixed effects in addition to 
industry-year fixed effects."

Response:
We appreciate this suggestion. However, after careful consideration, 
we believe that including firm FE alongside industry-year FE may not be 
appropriate in our setting for the following reasons:

① Our key explanatory variable (policy treatment) varies at the 
   industry-year level but NOT at the firm level within each cell. 
   Including firm FE would absorb all between-firm variation, leaving 
   no identifying variation for our variable of interest.

② This is a well-known issue discussed in Bertrand et al. (2004), who 
   note that when the treatment variable is at a higher aggregation level 
   than the fixed effects, the estimator may suffer from incidental 
   parameters problem.

That said, we have added a robustness check using alternative clustering 
(new Table A5, Column 4) where we cluster standard errors at the 
industry level rather than the firm level, and results remain consistent.

We hope this explanation clarifies our modeling choice.

✅ 回复的最佳实践 vs ❌ 应避免的错误

应该这样做

  • ✅ 以"We thank the reviewer for..."开头，保持礼貌和专业
  • ✅ 逐条回复，不要遗漏任何一条意见
  • ✅ 明确指出修改了文稿的哪些位置（页码+行号）
  • ✅ 对难以完全满足的意见，给出合理的解释+替代方案
  • ✅ 用不同颜色或字体高亮修改内容（方便审稿人核对）
  • ✅ 保持谦逊但有底气——你可以不同意，但要礼貌且有理据
  • ✅ 提交前请同学/导师帮忙检查回复质量

避免这些错误

  • ❌ 复制粘贴式的笼统回复（"We have addressed all comments"）
  • ❌ 与审稿人争辩或表现出防御性态度
  • ❌ 只说"我们修改了"但不说明具体改了什么
  • ❌ 忽略你不喜欢的意见（审稿人会注意到的！）
  • ❌ 回复信比原论文还长（审稿人没时间看小说）
  • ❌ 修改后的版本引入了新的问题
  • ❌ 错过了期刊规定的回复截止日期
